iPhone 17 Pro Max

Price: ৳1,95,000 · Brand: Apple

Last year's top iPhone is still one of the best phones you can buy, and the price has come down a lot since launch. You get a 6.9-inch 120Hz display, the A19 Pro chip and a triple 48MP camera system. Camera Control gives you a physical shutter and zoom slider on the side, and the Action button can be set to open the torch, a voice memo or any shortcut you like. For most people the jump to the 18 Pro Max is small, so this is the smart buy.

Specifications

  • Display: 6.9" LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED, 120Hz
  • Chip: A19 Pro (3nm), 6-core CPU, 6-core GPU
  • Camera: Triple 48MP rear, 18MP front
  • Features: Camera Control, Action button, Face ID, USB-C, IP68
