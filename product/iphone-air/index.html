iPhone Air

Price: ৳1,66,500 · Brand: Apple

The thinnest iPhone Apple has made, and it still has a large 6.9-inch display. It feels very light in the hand and slips into any pocket. There is one 48MP Fusion camera on the back instead of three, which covers everyday photos and 2x shots well. Inside is the same A19 Pro family chip as the Pro models, so performance is not the compromise here. Battery life is shorter than the Pro Max, so heavy users may want a MagSafe battery pack.

Specifications

  • Display: 6.9" LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED, 120Hz
  • Chip: A19 Pro (3nm), 6-core CPU, 5-core GPU
  • Camera: 48MP Fusion rear, 18MP front
  • Features: Camera Control, Action button, Face ID, USB-C, IP68
