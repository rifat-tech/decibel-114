Apple Pencil (USB-C)

Price: ৳9,100 · Brand: Apple

The simplest and most affordable Apple Pencil. It pairs and charges through USB-C and works with every iPad that has a USB-C port. It is pixel-precise and great for notes and marking up PDFs. It does not detect pressure, so serious artists should look at Apple Pencil Pro.

Specifications

  • Connectivity: Bluetooth
  • Charging: USB-C
  • Battery: Up to 12 hours
  • Compatibility: All USB-C iPads
