iPhone 15 Plus

Price: ৳1,26,500 · Brand: Apple

A large 6.7-inch iPhone with 256GB of storage, priced well below the newer models. The 48MP main camera takes detailed photos, and Dynamic Island shows timers, calls and deliveries at the top of the screen. It charges over USB-C, so it shares a cable with an iPad or MacBook. A sensible pick for parents, or anyone who wants a big screen and lots of space without paying flagship prices.
